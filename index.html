autofill-me privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

autofill-me is a browser extension that fills job application forms from a profile you save in it. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What the extension stores

  • The profile you enter or import: name, contact details, address, work history, education, compensation, notice period, links, screening answers and any sensitive details you choose to turn on.
  • Documents you add, such as your resume and cover letter.
  • Your settings, answers you save for reuse, and fields you map on specific sites.

All of this is stored only in your browser, using the browser's extension storage and IndexedDB on your device.

What the extension sends

Nothing. autofill-me makes no network requests. It has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Your information is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The only time your information leaves the extension is when you ask it to fill a form: it then types your details into that web page, in your browser, exactly as if you had typed them. What happens when you submit that form is governed by the site's own privacy policy. The extension never submits a form for you.

Resume and LinkedIn import

When you import a resume PDF or a LinkedIn PDF export, the file is read on your computer by code inside the extension. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Sensitive information

Details such as date of birth, gender, government ID numbers, family names and equal-opportunity answers are never filled unless you turn each one on, and they are always shown for your review.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any part of your profile in the extension's settings.
  • You can download a backup file and restore it later. The backup is a file on your computer; the extension doesn't keep copies elsewhere.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

  • storage: keeps your profile and settings in your browser.
  • activeTab and scripting: fill the page you're on when you click Fill.
  • Job sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Zoho, iCIMS, LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri): shows a Fill button on application forms and fills the next step of multi-step applications. Nothing is filled until you click.
  • debugger (optional, off unless you turn it on): types into fields that ignore scripted input.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with the extension's update, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this repository, or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
